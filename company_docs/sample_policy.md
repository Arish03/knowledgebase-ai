# Company Leave & Time-Off Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies to:** All full-time employees

---

## 1. Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month of service.

### Carry-Over

- Unused leave may be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of **5 days**.
- Carry-over days must be used by **March 31** of the following year, or they expire.

### Requesting Leave

- Submit leave requests via the internal HR portal at least **5 working days** in advance.
- Leave of 5 or more consecutive days requires **manager approval** plus **HR notification**.
- The company reserves the right to deny requests during critical project milestones.

---

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees receive **12 days** of paid sick leave per year.

- A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding **2 consecutive days**.
- Unused sick leave does **not** carry over or get paid out.

---

## 3. Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, subject to manager approval.

### Eligibility

- Employees must have completed their **probation period** (typically 3 months).
- Roles requiring on-site presence (e.g., facilities, lab work) may be exempt.

### Guidelines

- Core working hours are **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** — employees must be available during this window.
- A stable internet connection and a private workspace are required.
- Employees must use the company **VPN** for all work-related access.

---

## 4. Public Holidays

The company observes **10 public holidays** per year, as published on the HR portal each December for the following year. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the preceding Friday or following Monday is observed instead.

---

## 5. Parental Leave

- **Maternity leave:** 16 weeks paid leave.
- **Paternity leave:** 4 weeks paid leave.
- Leave must begin within **2 weeks** of the birth or adoption date.
- Extension requests are handled case-by-case by HR.

---

## 6. Expense Reimbursement

Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved business expenses.

- Submit claims within **30 days** of the expense via the finance portal.
- Receipts are required for all claims above **$25**.
- Travel expenses must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager.
- The maximum daily meal allowance during business travel is **$60**.

---

## 7. Code of Conduct — IT Security

- Employees must complete **annual IT security training** by December 31 each year.
- Passwords must be at least **12 characters** and changed every **90 days**.
- Use of unauthorized software or cloud storage for company data is **strictly prohibited**.
- Report suspected security incidents to security@company.com within **24 hours**.
